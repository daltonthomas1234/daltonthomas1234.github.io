Cases taken off the map

These are the cases that gave a district a color on the earlier version of this map and were then taken off, because the court document had not been read.

A later check looked for the opinion. 26 were retrieved, read, and put back on the map. 4 were not retrieved, so they still do not color a district. 2 were read and left off the map. The note on each one says why.

    D’Agostino v. Circle K Stores, No. 2:26-cv-01225 (D. Ariz. Apr. 23, 2026)

    Text treated as a callRead, and back on the map

    The order was retrieved from GovInfo and read. It is the document now used to color the District of Arizona. The order rejected the argument that a text is not a telephone call and left the § 227(c) counts in the case.

    GovInfo order
