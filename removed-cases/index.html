  • McGonigle v. Office Depot, LLC, No. 25-80069 (S.D. Fla. Nov. 25, 2025)

    Text treated as a call

    The order was retrieved and read. Judge William P. Dimitrouleas denied a contested motion for judgment on the pleadings and held that a text message is a call for the purpose of the TCPA.     Court order

  • Anthony v. Brian Marketing Group, No. 24-80800 (S.D. Fla. Sept. 11, 2026)

    Text not treated as a call

    The order was retrieved and read. Judge Aileen M. Cannon denied the plaintiff’s motion for a default judgment and closed the case. The defendant did not appear. The order holds that unwanted text messages to a cell phone cannot support a claim under § 227(c), and that § 227(c)(5) does not authorize a suit based on text messages rather than calls. It also holds that § 227(c) does not cover cellular subscribers. Together with McGonigle v. Office Depot, judges in the Southern District of Florida have gone both ways.

    Court order
