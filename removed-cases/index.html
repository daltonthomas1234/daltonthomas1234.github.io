Cases taken off the map

These are the cases that gave a district a color on the earlier version of this map and were then taken off, because the court document had not been read.

A later check looked for the opinion. 17 were retrieved, read, and put back on the map. 12 were not retrieved, so they still do not color a district. 3 were read and left off the map. The note on each one says why.

Checked October 24, 2026. This page is not a complete search, and it is not legal advice.
